## How to use each day

1. **Listen** — once for meaning (no transcript).
2. **Read** transcript — mark stress and linked words.
3. **Shadow** — 2–3 passes (pause–repeat or 0.5 s behind).
4. **Record** — 30–60 s retell or read one paragraph aloud.
5. **Log** one sound or phrase in the week table below.
